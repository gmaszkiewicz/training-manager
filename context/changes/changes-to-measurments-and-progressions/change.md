## Notes

The changes mainly concern the Top progress tile and shipping it as release 0.9, a release candidate.

1. Replace the Circumferences choice box with a filter, and put that filter on every chart in Top progress.
2. Week, Month, Year and Years become a choice box with arrows, the same as the picker on the left.
3. Make the week, month, year and last-5-years choice boxes one size in every tile, including Your measurements, Weekly, Monthly, Year and Progress over the past 5 years. All of them have the same width: the width of the longest possible value, which will be "September 2026".
4. In every tile, the choice box offers only values that have entries in the measurements journal. If there are none, the choice box and arrows are grey with a "no measurements" message, as now.
5. Visualize, Clear and "x of 15 selected" sit below the measurements table, on the left, even when pagination appears. When pagination is visible, add a divider line as well.
6. In Top progress, the period choice box sits on the right.
7. Mark Top progress as 0.9 (RC). RC gets a yellow badge on the tile with the same description, "Release candidate - it may contain bugs", and is on by default.
8. In Top progress, Month view on the weight and measurement axis shows the day with the biggest progress, and labels only the days with progress, but at least 3 (first, middle and last day of the month) if there are none.
9. The previous Top progress stays selectable and looks as it does now.
